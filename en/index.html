How to use & Tabata / HIIT basics

How do I use this interval timer?

Open Settings, enter your work time, rest time, sets per round and rounds, then press Start. The timer counts down and tells you when to switch with beeps, ticking and an English voice. The settings bar shows your workout at a glance, and you can drag the progress bar to jump to any point.

How do I set up Tabata?

Tabata is 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest, repeated 8 times for a total of 4 minutes. Set Work 20, Rest between sets 10, Sets per round 8, Rounds 1 and Rest after round 0.

What is the difference between HIIT and Tabata?

HIIT (high-intensity interval training) alternates hard effort with rest, at whatever ratio you choose. Tabata is one specific form of it, fixed at 20 seconds on and 10 seconds off.
